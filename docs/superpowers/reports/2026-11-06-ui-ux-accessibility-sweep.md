# UI/UX and accessibility sweep

Report only: nothing in this list has been changed. Pick what you want fixed and it becomes a small plan of its own.

Taken on the branch at `814ca8c` (the header menu, Reset word icon, red confirmations and 30-row list are in), against the built app.

## Summary

- **No accessibility rule failure was found by axe-core in 100 page and dialog states**, light and dark, at 1280px and 390px (touch). The one report is a missing `<h1>` on the not-found page.
- **Every colour pair passes** its WCAG ratio, except the pale `--rule` borders (1.29:1) that outline the quiet buttons.
- **Keyboard:** every tab stop on the home page and on three kinds of card shows a focus indicator, order is sensible, nothing traps, and nothing hides under the fixed action bar.
- **What needs a decision:** one colour-only status in the phone's word list (Serious), four destructive actions that do not ask first (a decision for you), and a list of small things.
- An independent reviewer checked this report and found three problems I had missed (findings 18 to 20), two understated ones (7 and 17) and two claims that were too broad (the action bar and the alerts); all are corrected here.

Severity: **Serious** = a person cannot tell or cannot do something; **Minor** = a rough edge with a workaround; **Taste** = your call, nothing is broken. Effort: S under an hour, M a few hours.

## Not accessibility, but worth knowing

- **Five dialogs repeat the same open and close effect** (`FormsDialog`, `ResetDialog`, `Backups`, `UnusedAudio`, `Shortcuts`), and the three-dot icon is drawn in both `Clip.tsx` and `Menu.tsx`. A `useModal(open)` hook would remove the copies. (S)
- **`keys.cy.ts` fails sometimes**, always the test "keeps moving forward through a list when every step creates the card of the next word", which times out on word 190. It failed 2 of 8 single-spec runs on `294fa48` (before this plan) and 1 of 6 on this branch, so it is old and not made worse. It looks like a hydration race: the test types Ctrl+→ right after `hydrated(Hint)`. I can fix it if you want. (S to M)
- **`scale.cy.ts` "ten rows… without scrolling"** depends on which card was edited last: run after `list`, `look` and `layout` it fails (1133.67px against 1100px), also without any of this work. It passes alone and in the normal full order. (S)

## Checked and found fine

- **axe-core 4.14, 100 states**: home (plain, search, bad search, sorted, last page, no results), card (bare, Details open, full and approved, in progress, ready, outside the list, example being edited, clip options open), Forms and Reset dialogs, not-found, the menu open on the home page and on a card, Keyboard shortcuts, Backups (list, confirm Restore, confirm Delete), Delete unused audio, and the export message showing, each at 1280px and at 390px (touch), in light and dark. Tags: WCAG 2.0, 2.1, 2.2 A and AA, and best practice.
- **Contrast** of every text and component pair in both schemes (computed from the tokens): all pass except `--rule` as a border (finding 9). `quiet` on the hover background is the tightest text pair at 4.69 and 4.82.
- **Keyboard:** all tab stops show an outline or box-shadow; order is wordmark, Menu, Reset word, headword, clip controls, fields, example, Details, Approve; Tab leaves the page after the last control; none of the controls I tabbed through is covered by the fixed action bar at 390×844 and 1280×360 (`scroll-padding-bottom` is set); the example editor at a short window is an open question below.
- **Focus return:** after every dialog and the menu close, by any route, the focus is back on the opener (checked in real Chromium by Tasks 1 to 5, with real key and mouse input). Other moments where focus is lost are finding 19.
- **Reflow** at 320px: no sideways scroll on the home page, cards, the open menu or the Backups dialog. **Text spacing** (line height 1.5, letter 0.12em, word 0.16em): no horizontal overflow.
- **Structure:** landmarks (banner, main, search, the Status and Pages navigations, regions, toolbar), one sr-only `<h1>` per page, h2 inside dialogs, `lang="en"` on the page and `lang="ar" dir="rtl"` on Arabic, page titles on cards ("Word 6 · Mufradat").
- **Dialogs** are labelled; confirmation dialogs start with the focus on Cancel; most errors are `role="alert"` (two are not: finding 18); the search error sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`; the card's "Saved" status is a live region that is always present.
- **Motion:** transitions are short and switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; nothing autoplays. **Zoom:** the viewport tag allows zooming and sizes are in `rem`.
- **Buttons:** every control has an accessible name that contains its visible text, except that "#" is named "Rank (#)" (axe could not tell; the name does contain "#").

## Open questions

- **Is the example editor partly hidden by the action bar in a short window?** The reviewer measured it at 203–378px with the bar starting at 296px (1280×360) when reached by Tab, which would be a partial cover (it still passes WCAG 2.4.11, and only fails the stricter 2.4.12). I measured it at 104–279px with the bar at 297px, fully visible. The scroll position depends on the route taken, so the two measurements may both be right; the Mark target button and the help line below the editor are the parts at risk. Not resolved.

## Limits

- No real screen reader, voice control or switch device was used; automated tools and a read of the code find a minority of problems.
- Chromium only. The anchored popover (`position-area`) and the browser's own focus return after a popover are Chrome-based behaviours; Firefox and Safari are unverified.
- Audio playback, Mistral and Google text-to-speech flows were not exercised (no credentials, and they cost money).
- Findings 2 and 10 come from reading the code and the accessibility tree, not from watching a person use them.
- Items marked as measured by the reviewer (parts of findings 17, 19 and 20) I did not re-measure.
